Scroll Bureau — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

Scroll Bureau does not collect your data, does not have accounts, and has no servers. Everything it shows you is calculated on your phone, from data that stays on your phone. There is no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

Who this policy is from

Scroll Bureau is published by Sarah Studios. Questions about this policy can be sent to sarahkwok132@gmail.com.

What the app stores

The app saves the settings you enter, in its own private storage area on your device. That is:

  • The pay figure you enter — either an annual salary or an hourly rate — and, optionally, how many weeks a year you work
  • Your chosen currency
  • Your daily scroll allowance, in minutes
  • Which apps you have chosen to track
  • Whether sample-data mode is switched on

This information is never sent anywhere. It is not transmitted to the developer, and there is nowhere for it to be transmitted to — the app has no backend service of any kind.

What the app reads

To show you how long you have spent in the apps you track, Scroll Bureau uses Android's Usage Access permission (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS). This is a special permission that you grant yourself in Android Settings, and that you can revoke at any time in the same place. The app cannot grant it to itself.

Screen time figures are read from the Android system each time you open the app or the widget refreshes, used to work out the number on screen, and then discarded. The app keeps no history and builds no record of your usage over time. Close the app and nothing about your activity has been written down.

The app also reads the list of apps installed on your device, so that the app picker in Settings can show you real names and icons. That list is never transmitted or stored.

The app cannot send data anywhere

This is worth stating precisely rather than as a promise. An Android app can only use the network if it declares the INTERNET permission. Scroll Bureau does not declare it. The app is therefore technically incapable of making a network connection, whatever it might otherwise claim. You can verify this yourself from the app's permission list.

The one exception: Android's own backup

Android includes a system-level backup feature that is operated by Google, not by this app. If you have device backup switched on in your Android settings, the settings listed above — your pay figure, currency, allowance and tracked app list — may be included in your device's backup to your own Google account, and restored when you set up a new phone. This is what makes your settings survive an upgrade.

This backup is performed by the Android operating system. The data goes to your own Google account, governed by Google's Privacy Policy, and is encrypted using your device PIN, pattern or password on Android 9 and above. The developer of this app has no access to it. Your screen time figures are not part of this, because the app never stores them in the first place.

You can turn device backup off entirely in Android Settings, under Google › Backup.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it has stored. You can also clear it without uninstalling, from Android Settings › Apps › Scroll Bureau › Storage › Clear data. Because nothing is held anywhere else, there is no account to close and no deletion request to make of anyone.

Children

Scroll Bureau is designed for adults working out what their own time is worth, and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.
