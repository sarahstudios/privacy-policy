Bark is a small social app for arranging dog walks with people you know. This policy explains what it stores, why, who else can see it, and how to get rid of it.

Bark is provided by [LEGAL NAME / TRADING NAME] (“we”, “us”), of [POSTAL ADDRESS]. For UK and EU data protection law we are the data controller for the information described here.

Questions, requests, or complaints: [CONTACT EMAIL].

The short version

  • Bark stores your account details, the plans you post, and who your friends and packs are. That is essentially all of it.
  • Bark never accesses your device’s location. It doesn’t ask for the permission and can’t read your GPS. Every location in the app is a place you typed in and chose yourself.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no profiling. We don’t sell or share your data with anyone for marketing.
  • Other Bark users see your name, username, avatar, and the plans you send to packs they belong to.

1. What we collect

Everything below is given to us by you, in the app. Bark collects nothing in the background.

Account

  • Email address — to sign in, verify your account, and reset your password.
  • Password — handled by our authentication provider and stored only as a salted hash. We never see it.
  • Display name and username — your username is how other people find and add you.

Profile and settings

  • Your chosen avatar, colour theme, and cat-mode preference.
  • Your home location: the place name you picked plus its coordinates. This is used to work out which howls are near enough to show you.
  • Your nearby howl radius.
  • Your notification preferences (barks, howls, friend requests).

Plans you post

For each bark or howl you create: the date and time, the place name and its coordinates, your optional message, whether it’s a bark or a howl and how long it lasts, which packs you sent it to, and whether it was cancelled. We also record when other people mark themselves as coming.

Your social graph

Your friends, friend requests you’ve sent and received, the packs you’ve created and who is in them, and any users you have muted.

Notifications

If you turn on push notifications we store a device notification token so messages can reach your phone. It identifies the app installation, not you personally, and is deleted when you turn notifications off, sign out, or uninstall.

Feedback

If you send feedback from Settings, we store the message and your user ID so we can make sense of it and follow up.

What we do not collect

  • Your device’s GPS or network location, at any time.
  • Contacts, photos, files, microphone, or camera.
  • Advertising identifiers.
  • Analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
  • Automatic crash reports.

2. Why we’re allowed to use it (UK/EU legal bases)

  • To perform our contract with you — running your account, showing you plans, delivering barks and howls to the people you sent them to. Without this data the app cannot function.
  • Our legitimate interests — keeping the service secure, preventing abuse, and improving Bark using the feedback you choose to send.
  • Your consent — push notifications, which you turn on and can turn off at any time in Settings or your phone’s settings.

3. Who can see your information

Other Bark users

Bark is a social app, so some information is shared by design:

  • Your display name, username, and avatar are visible to people who search for your username and to people you’re connected to.
  • A bark or howl is visible to the members of the packs you send it to, and to nobody else. It includes the place, time, and any message you added.
  • People in those packs can see who has said they’re coming.
  • When you send a friend request, the recipient sees your name and username.

Please treat a plan’s location as public to the pack you send it to. Don’t put anything in a message you wouldn’t want everyone in that pack to read.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Legal requests

We may disclose information if we are legally required to, or where it is necessary to protect someone’s safety or our legal rights.

4. How long we keep it

  • Account and profile data — until you delete your account.
  • Barks and howls — kept until you delete your account. Older plans stop appearing in the app roughly three days after they finish, but remain in the database until then.
  • Notification tokens — removed when you disable notifications, sign out, or uninstall; stale tokens are cleared automatically when delivery fails.
  • Feedback — kept while it’s useful for improving the app.

5. Deleting your account

Email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will remove your account, profile, plans, friendships, pack memberships, and notification tokens within 30 days.

Note that plans you sent to a pack may remain visible in the app to that pack’s other members until they age out normally, and messages you sent as feedback are anonymised rather than reconstructed.

You can also sign out at any time from Settings, which stops the app on that device without deleting anything.

6. Your rights

If you’re in the UK or EEA, you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, receive a copy in a portable format, and withdraw consent for notifications. Similar rights apply in some other regions.

To exercise any of these, email [CONTACT EMAIL]. We’ll respond within one month.

You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority. In the UK that’s the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

7. Security

Access to your data is enforced at the database level, so the app can only ever read rows you’re entitled to see. Connections are encrypted in transit, and passwords are stored as salted hashes by our authentication provider. Your sign-in session is stored in your device’s protected app storage.

No system is perfectly secure, but we don’t hold payment details, government identifiers, or precise device location — there is deliberately very little here to lose.

8. Children

Bark is not intended for children under [13 / 16 — confirm], and we don’t knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we’ll remove it.
