Delete your Bark account

This permanently deletes your Bark account and everything attached to it. It cannot be undone, and it does not require you to have the app installed.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile — name, username, avatar, location and notification settings
  • Every bark and howl you created, and everyone's replies to them
  • Your replies to other people's barks and howls
  • Packs you own, and your membership of other people's packs
  • Your friendships, pending friend requests and mutes — removed for the other person too
  • Push notification tokens for your devices
  • Any feedback you sent us
  • Your login itself

What we keep

Nothing. Once deletion finishes, no data linked to your account remains in the live database. Routine encrypted backups may hold a copy for up to 7 days before they expire on their normal rotation.
